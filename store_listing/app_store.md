## Description
ExcelMind Academic Companion is a modern student evaluation, computer-based testing (CBT), and digital learning ecosystem engineered for secondary schools and higher education candidates.

### Features:
* 24/7 AI Tutor with image recognition for complex equations and science diagrams.
* Timed CBT exam simulator mirroring actual WAEC & JAMB UTME environments.
* Offline lecture notes and downloadable revision packs.
* Real-time student performance tracking and automated grade predictions.
* Multi-portal access for Students, Teachers, Parents, and Administrators.
