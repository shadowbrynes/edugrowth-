## Full Description (Max 4000 chars)
ExcelMind Academic Companion is the premier digital education evaluation and learning companion platform designed specifically for Nigerian secondary schools and university-bound students.

Aligned with the statutory Nigerian Educational Research and Development Council (NERDC) national standards, WAEC (WASSCE), NECO (SSCE), and JAMB (UTME) syllabi, ExcelMind combines smart learning management, computer-based testing (CBT), interactive video lessons, and 24/7 AI tutoring into a single mobile app.

### Key Features:
* 🤖 **AI Academic Tutor (Powered by Gemini AI)**: Instant homework help, question photo recognition, step-by-step mathematical proofs, and automated weekly revision plans.
* 🖥️ **National Standard CBT Practice**: Realistic timed computer-based exam engine for WAEC, NECO, JAMB, and BECE with instant scoring and examiner rationales.
* 📚 **Interactive Learning Hub**: High-definition video masterclasses, downloadable PDF lecture handbooks, audio podcasts, and peer discussion forums.
* 🇳🇬 **Automated Curriculum Assignment**: Instant department streaming (Science, Commercial, Arts) with automated core and elective subject allocation.
* 🏆 **Gamified Rewards & Streaks**: Earn XP reward points, maintain study streaks, and unlock achievement badges as you master topics.
* 👨‍👩‍👧 **Parent & Guardian Portal**: Verified monthly AI academic reports, attendance monitoring, and direct teacher communication.
* 📶 **Offline Learning Support**: Download notes, textbooks, and CBT question palettes for uninterrupted study on slow internet or offline.
